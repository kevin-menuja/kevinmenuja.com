# Continuous site audio

The original mastered owner voice MP3 remains byte-identical to the prior revision. The shared root layout hosts a single native audio element, attempts audible playback immediately on entry, then switches to the original 60-second synthesized instrumental AAC track at the end of narration. Music plays with native looping and persists across Next.js client-side navigation. A full reload after introduction completion starts the music again from the beginning. Session storage tracks completed narration. Sound is enabled and unmuted on each full document load, ignoring any legacy saved mute setting. Muting persists across client navigation within that document, but resets on a full reload or fresh visit. The accessible navbar sound control permits pause/mute and resume. No third-party audio service or dependency was added.

The soundtrack was generated by scripts/generate-ambient.py using original sustained chords and gentle keys, with a source peak of about -19.6 dBFS and 24 kHz mono AAC output. It is intentionally played after, not over, the voice. Browser autoplay and OS media policies remain authoritative: audible autoplay cannot be guaranteed without interaction. Music can also be interrupted by browser/OS suspension or network conditions. The script displayed on the homepage remains the proposed text, not a verified transcription.

Verification: final lint, production build, TypeScript, git diff whitespace check, full 34/34 regression suite, and production dependency audit (0 reported vulnerabilities) passed. scripts/check-site-audio.mjs verified actual voice playback, transition, loop wrap and mute in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit. Chromium no-click playback was verified with autoplay explicitly permitted in an isolated browser test; blocked autoplay was tested separately with a simulated rejection and manual activation. The regression suite also checks persistent player identity across navigation, saved mute preference and responsive/accessibility states. No physical device or subjective soundtrack listening acceptance, fresh Lighthouse measurement, or production deployment is claimed. Preview branch only.
